SSS Benefits Overview

Sickness Benefit
90% of daily salary credit × sick days. Min. 3 days, max. 120 days/year. Need 3 months contribution in 12 months before semester of sickness.
Daily: 90% of (MSC ÷ 30)

Maternity Benefit
105 days (singleton) or 120 days (multiple birth). Solo parent: +15 days. Need 3 months contribution in prior 12 months.
100% of avg daily salary credit × days

Disability Benefit
Monthly pension or lump sum based on degree of disability and credited contributions.
Based on MSC and credited years of service

Retirement Benefit
Age 60 (optional) or 65 (mandatory). Need minimum 120 posted contributions. Monthly pension or lump sum.
Min. ₱2,000/month pension

Death / Funeral Benefit
Monthly pension for primary beneficiaries. Funeral grant for burial expenses.
Funeral grant: ₱20,000 – ₱40,000

Calamity Loan
