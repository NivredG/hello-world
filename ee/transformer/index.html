🧠 Mnemonics para sa Board Exam

N-I-V-I
N₁/N₂ = V₁/V₂ = I₂/I₁
Tandaan: Current ay BALIGTAD — I₂ nasa itaas, I₁ nasa ibaba!
"NIVI" — N una, V gitna, I baligtad sa dulo.

🔺 Step-Up vs Step-Down Trick

"Step UP ang Voltage = Step DOWN ang Current"
Kung N₂ > N₁ → Step-UP transformer → V₂ mas mataas, I₂ mas mababa.
Kung N₂ < N₁ → Step-DOWN transformer → V₂ mas mababa, I₂ mas mataas.

Bakit? Conservation of energy! P₁ ≈ P₂ (ideal transformer), kaya kung V tumaas, I dapat bumaba para manatiling equal ang power.

🔋 Efficiency Trick

"η = Output ÷ Input, losses nasa denominator via OUTPUT"
η = P_out / P_in = P_out / (P_out + P_losses)

Board exam trick: Kung binibigay ang losses at output power:
P_in = P_out + P_cu + P_fe
η = P_out / (P_out + P_cu + P_fe) × 100%

Max efficiency condition: P_cu = P_fe (copper loss = iron loss)
Sa board exam, itanong nila ang load fraction para max efficiency: x = √(P_fe / P_cu_FL)

📈 Voltage Regulation Mnemonic

"No Load MINUS Full Load, OVER Full Load"
VR = (V_NL − V_FL) / V_FL × 100%

Tandaan: "NL minus FL, over FL"
Palaging positive para sa lagging power factor (normal load).
Negative VR = leading PF (capacitive load) — rare sa board pero posible.

⚠️ Common Mistake – HUWAG GAWIN!

Mali: Gamitin ang N₁/N₂ = I₁/I₂ (MALI ito!)
Tama: N₁/N₂ = I₂/I₁ (BALIGTAD ang current!)

Maraming examinees ang nalilito dito! Ang turns ratio at voltage ratio ay direct proportion, pero ang current ratio ay inverse proportion.

PH Context: Sa Meralco 13.2kV transformer, ang primary current ay maliit (mataas ang voltage), secondary current ay malaki (mababa ang voltage). Ito ang proof na baligtad talaga!
📝 Sample PRC EE Board Problems
Problem 1 – Turns Ratio & Current
Isang transformer ang may 2,000 primary turns at 500 secondary turns. Kung ang primary voltage ay 13,200 V at ang primary current ay 10 A, hanapin ang secondary voltage at secondary current.
1
Turns ratio: a = N₁/N₂ = 2000/500 = 4
2
Secondary voltage: V₂ = V₁/a = 13,200/4 = 3,300 V
3
Secondary current (BALIGTAD!): I₂ = I₁ × a = 10 × 4 = 40 A
✅ V₂ = 3,300 V | I₂ = 40 A (Step-DOWN transformer)
Problem 2 – Efficiency with Losses
Ang isang 25 kVA, 13,200/230 V transformer ay may copper loss na 320 W at iron loss na 180 W sa full load. Ang power factor ng load ay 0.85 lagging. Kalkulahin ang efficiency sa full load.
1
Output power: P_out = kVA × PF = 25,000 × 0.85 = 21,250 W
2
Total losses: P_loss = P_cu + P_fe = 320 + 180 = 500 W
3
Input power: P_in = 21,250 + 500 = 21,750 W
4
Efficiency: η = 21,250/21,750 × 100% = 97.70%
✅ η = 97.70% — Magandang transformer performance!
Problem 3 – Voltage Regulation
Ang secondary no-load voltage ng isang transformer ay 240 V. Sa full load, bumaba ito sa 228 V. Kalkulahin ang voltage regulation.
1
Gamiting ang mnemonic: "NL minus FL, over FL"
2
VR = (V_NL − V_FL) / V_FL × 100%
3
VR = (240 − 228) / 228 × 100% = 12/228 × 100% = 5.26%
✅ VR = 5.26% — Acceptable para sa distribution transformer

💚 Power Mnemonics

Trick: "Ang output ay laging mas maliit sa input — may losses palagi!"

"NL − FL / FL = VR"

Trick: "NL nasa taas, FL nasa lahat" — NL ang numerator at denominator, FL pang denominator lang.

📚 Real ECE Board Mnemonics

"HEMEC" — Transformer Loss Types
Hysteresis Loss — core loss, proportional sa frequency at flux density (Steinmetz equation)
Eddy Current Loss — core loss, proportional sa f² at B²
Mechanical Loss — (para sa motors/generators; sa transformer ay negligible)
Electrical/Copper Loss — I²R sa winding resistance, nagbabago ayon sa load
Core/Stray Loss — iba pang miscellaneous losses
"HEMEC sounds like 'may sakit' — para di malimutan na may losses ang transformer!"
Board tip: Iron/Core losses (H+E) = constant kahit mag-iba ang load.
Copper losses = variable, depende sa I² (load current squared).
"NIVI" Expanded — Turns Ratio Master Formula
N₁/N₂ = V₁/V₂ = I₂/I₁ ← (BALIGTAD ang I!)
Mnemonic: "NIVI — N at V ay direct, I ay inverse!"
Quick check: Step-UP transformer (N₂ > N₁) → V₂ ↑ subalit I₂ ↓ — conservation of power!
Max Efficiency Condition — "Copper = Iron"
Max efficiency ay nangyayari kapag: P_cu = P_fe
Load fraction para sa max η: x = √(P_fe / P_cu_FL)
Mnemonic: "Mag-match ang Copper at Iron para maximum ang efficiency — tulad ng perfect na kasal!"
Board formula: η_max occurs when variable loss = fixed loss (Copper = Iron/Core loss)
