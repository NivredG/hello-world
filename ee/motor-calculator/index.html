🧠Board Exam Mnemonics
S
"Sixty-Forty Rule" — Slip formula: s = (Ns − Nr) / Ns × 100%. Remember: Synchronous MINUS rotor, OVER synchronous. Palagi Ns ang denominator!
H
"746 Watts" — 1 HP = 746W = 0.746 kW. Para sa quick estimate: 1 HP ≈ 0.75 kW (close enough pero gamitin ang exact na 0.746 sa exam!)
P
"PIN = Pout ÷ η" — Input power = output power divided by efficiency. Kung 90% efficient at 10 kW output → Pin = 10/0.9 = 11.11 kW
N
"120f over P" — Synchronous speed Ns = 120 × frequency / poles. Sa PH: 60Hz standard. 4-pole motor → Ns = 120×60/4 = 1800 RPM
T
"TAv = P" (Torque × Angular velocity = Power) — T = P / ω = P / (2πN/60). Result is in N·m. Huwag kalimutang i-convert ang RPM to rad/s!
√3
"Root Three Factor" — 3-phase power: P = √3 × V × I × PF. Ang √3 ≈ 1.732. Sa calculator: mag-type ng 1.732 kung wala square root button sa exam room.
Sample Board Problems
📝 Problem 1
A 3-phase, 10 HP, 460V induction motor runs at 1,746 RPM. PF = 0.85, η = 0.92. Ano ang full load current?
Step 1: I-convert HP to kW: 10 HP × 0.746 = 7.46 kW (output power)
Step 2: I-solve ang input power: Pin = Pout/η = 7.46/0.92 = 8.109 kW
Step 3: I-apply ang 3-phase FLC formula: I = P/(√3 × V × PF) = 8,109/(1.732 × 460 × 0.85)
Step 4: I = 8,109 / 677.3 = 11.97 A ≈ 12 A
✅ Sagot: ≈ 12 Amperes
📝 Problem 2
A 4-pole, 60Hz induction motor has a rotor speed of 1,710 RPM. What is the percent slip?
Step 1: I-solve ang synchronous speed: Ns = 120f/P = (120 × 60)/4 = 1,800 RPM
Step 2: I-apply ang slip formula: s = (Ns − Nr)/Ns × 100%
Step 3: s = (1800 − 1710)/1800 × 100% = 90/1800 × 100% = 5%
✅ Sagot: 5% slip (typical para sa squirrel cage)
📝 Problem 3
A motor delivers 5 HP at 1,750 RPM. Ano ang developed torque sa N·m?
Step 1: I-convert HP to Watts: 5 HP × 746 = 3,730 W = 3.73 kW
Step 2: I-convert RPM to rad/s: ω = 2π × N/60 = 2π × 1750/60 = 183.26 rad/s
Step 3: T = P/ω = 3,730/183.26 = 20.35 N·m
✅ Sagot: ≈ 20.35 N·m
⚠️Common Board Exam Traps!
🪤
HP vs kW: Palaging tanungin ang sarili — HP o kW ang hinahanap? Malaking trap ito! Kung "output" ang tanong → HP muna i-convert sa kW bago kalkulahin.
🪤
Input vs Output: "Motor efficiency" means Pin at labas, Pout sa shaft. Huwag i-reverse! Pin > Pout palagi dahil may losses.
🪤
√3 Factor: Para sa 3-phase, may √3 = 1.732. Para sa 1-phase, WALA. Kung malito: tingnan ang "phase" sa problem.
🪤
Line vs Phase Voltage: Sa delta connection, Vline = Vphase. Sa wye, Vline = √3 × Vphase. Ang 460V sa exam ay LINE voltage.
🪤
Exact vs Approximate: 1 HP = 0.746 kW (exact). Huwag gumamit ng 0.75 sa final answer — 0.75 kW is ≈0.67% off pero marker-dependent.
🏭BEPZ/PEZA Factory Motor Sizing Context

Sa mga BEPZ (Bataan Export Processing Zone) at PEZA factories, ang karaniwang motor configurations ay:


Pangkalahatan na Setup:
• Supply: 460V, 3-phase, 60Hz (industrial standard PH)
• Motor types: Mostly SCIM (Squirrel Cage) dahil mababa ang maintenance
• Service factor: 1.15 (NEMA standard) — handa sa 115% load
• Protection: IP55 minimum sa factory floor (dust + water resistant)

Motor Sizing Rule (PEC Section 4.30):
• Branch circuit conductor: 125% ng FLC
• Overcurrent protection: 250% ng FLC (inverse time breaker)
• Sa exam: conductor = 1.25 × FLC, breaker = 2.5 × FLC

Halimbawa (10 HP, 460V, 3-phase):
• FLC ≈ 14A (NEC table)
• Conductor size: 14A × 1.25 = 17.5A → use #12 AWG (20A rated)
• Breaker: 14A × 2.5 = 35A → use 40A standard size

📚Real ECE Board Mnemonics
"Generightor" — Generator vs Motor
Gene-RIGHTOR = Generator converts mechanical → electrical energy
Motor = mechanical OUTPUT device (electrical → mechanical)
Trick: "Generightor generates electricity — Motor moves things!"
Board tip: Pag may "back EMF" sa problem → motor mode. Pag "EMF generated" → generator mode.
"Father over Mother equals Anak" — Turns/Voltage/Current
Father (N₁ primary turns) ÷ Mother (N₂ secondary) = Anak (ratio a)
N₁/N₂ = V₁/V₂ = I₂/I₁
Paalala: Current ay BALIGTAD! I₂ nasa taas ng ratio, I₁ nasa ibaba.
SHM Formula Mnemonic
x = A cos(ωt + φ)
"A Cos ang Omega Time Phase"
A = amplitude, ω = angular frequency (rad/s), φ = phase angle
ω = 2πf = 2π/T
Sa motor: ω ay rotor speed; sa SHM: ω ay oscillation frequency
120f/P Speed Trick
Ns = 120 × f / P
"Isang Dalawang Pulos — 120f sa Poles"
Sa PH (60 Hz): 2P=3600, 4P=1800, 6P=1200, 8P=900
Kabisahin ang 4 na values na ito — lagi itong tinatanong!
💡 CalTech tip: I-store ang √3 = 1.732050808 sa variable A sa simula ng exam.
Tapos gamitin: P ÷ (A × V × PF × η) para sa 3-phase FLC — walang re-type!

📋Board Exam CalTech Tips
🔑 SHIFT + CALC = SOLVE — Pinakamahalagang button sa board exam. Kahit anong equation na may unknown, SOLVE mode ang gagamitin. Practice mo ito!
🔑 Store intermediate results — Huwag mag-retype! Gamitin ang STO A–F para sa bawat sub-answer. Maiiwasan ang maling type-in.
🔑 Check mode bago kalkulahin — Siguruhing COMP mode (Mode 1) ang nakaset, hindi STAT o CMPLX mode. Kung may nakitang nakakaibang display, RESET ang mode: MODE 1
🔑 π at √3 — store agad pagpasok sa exam
√3 → STO A (i-set sa simula pa lang)
π ≈ 3.14159 → usually built-in na key (SHIFT EXP)
2π ÷ 60 ≈ 0.10472 → STO B (para sa torque problems)
🔑 Unit trap — palaging W hindi kW sa torque!
T = P(W) ÷ ω(rad/s) → sagot ay N·m
Kung P ay nasa kW → × 1000 muna bago ÷ ω
Common mistake: 15 kW ÷ ω = maling sagot! Dapat 15,000 W ÷ ω
