📝 Board Exam Tips — 3-Phase Power
🎯 Pro tip: Sa ECE board exam, laging tandaan: √3 ≈ 1.732. I-memorize mo na ang formula: P = √3 · VL · IL · PF. Ito ang pinaka-common na tanong!
1
⚡ Star (Y) Connected Load — BEPZ Factory Context
A Y-connected three-phase motor sa isang factory sa Bataan Economic Zone (BEPZ) ay may line voltage na 400V at line current na 25A. Ang power factor nito ay 0.85 lagging. Hanapin ang: (a) Real Power, (b) Reactive Power, (c) Apparent Power, at (d) PF angle.
Given:
VL = 400V, IL = 25A, PF = cos(θ) = 0.85 lagging

Step 1: Hanapin ang PF angle (θ)
θ = arccos(0.85) = 31.79°

Step 2: Apparent Power S
S = √3 × VL × IL = 1.732 × 400 × 25 = 17,320 VA ≈ 17.32 kVA
Step 3: Real Power P
P = S × cos(θ) = 17,320 × 0.85 = 14,722 W ≈ 14.72 kW
Step 4: Reactive Power Q
Q = S × sin(θ) = 17,320 × sin(31.79°) = 17,320 × 0.5268 ≈ 9,124 VAR
✅ Sagot: P = 14.72 kW | Q = 9.12 kVAR | S = 17.32 kVA | θ = 31.79°
2
△ Delta Connected — Y-to-Δ Conversion
A balanced Y-connected impedance na may Z_Y = 10∠30° Ω per phase ay kailangang i-convert sa equivalent Delta (Δ) connection. Pati na rin, kung ang line voltage ay 230V at PF = 0.866, ano ang total power ng Delta circuit?
Part A: Y to Delta Conversion
Z_Δ = 3 × Z_Y = 3 × (10∠30°) = 30∠30° Ω
Kaya ang bawat phase ng Delta ay may impedance na 30∠30° Ω.

Part B: Power Calculation (Δ connected)
VL = Vph = 230V (Delta configuration!)
Iph = Vph / |Z_Δ| = 230 / 30 = 7.667 A
IL = √3 × Iph = 1.732 × 7.667 = 13.28 A

PF = cos(30°) = 0.866
P = √3 × VL × IL × PF = 1.732 × 230 × 13.28 × 0.866 ≈ 4,592 W
O kaya direkta: P = 3 × Iph² × R = 3 × (7.667)² × (30×cos30°) = 3 × 58.78 × 25.98 ≈ 4,583 W
✅ Z_Δ = 30∠30° Ω | IL ≈ 13.28 A | P ≈ 4.59 kW
3
🏭 Industrial Load — Power Factor Correction
Ang isang industrial plant sa Mariveles, Bataan ay may 3-phase load: P = 150 kW, VL = 13.8 kV, at PF = 0.75 lagging. Gusto ng plant na i-improve ang PF sa 0.95 lagging. Gaano karaming kVAR ng capacitor bank ang kailangan?
Step 1: Original reactive power Q₁
S₁ = P / PF₁ = 150,000 / 0.75 = 200,000 VA = 200 kVA
θ₁ = arccos(0.75) = 41.41°
Q₁ = P × tan(θ₁) = 150 × tan(41.41°) = 150 × 0.8819 = 132.29 kVAR
Step 2: New reactive power Q₂ (at PF = 0.95)
θ₂ = arccos(0.95) = 18.19°
Q₂ = P × tan(θ₂) = 150 × tan(18.19°) = 150 × 0.3287 = 49.31 kVAR
Step 3: Capacitor bank required
Q_C = Q₁ - Q₂ = 132.29 - 49.31 = 82.98 kVAR ≈ 83 kVAR
✅ Kailangan ng ≈ 83 kVAR capacitor bank para ma-improve ang PF mula 0.75 → 0.95

🧠 Mnemonics — Tandaan Mo Na!
💡 Memory hack: Ang mga mnemonic na ito ay para sa ECE/EE board exam speed-solving. Taglish para mas madaling tandaan!
🌟 "Tres Pormula" — The Big 3
P · Q · S
Parang Quiero Siya — "Parang gusto siya" 😄
→ Tatlo silang magkakasama sa Power Triangle

P = Real (Totoo, nagagamit talaga) — W
Q = Reactive (Baliw, hindi nagagamit) — VAR
S = Apparent (Siya yung total, boss) — VA
S² = P² + Q² → "Pythagoras sila!"
⭐ "VILS" — Star Config Trick
Para sa Star (Y), tandaan ang "VILS":
Voltage → Increases (VL = √3 × Vph) — Dumadami ang voltage!
Icurrent → Likha (IL = Iph) — Pareho lang! Same current.
Star = Voltage ang nag-multiply ng √3

Kaya sa Star: "Voltage ang nagmamagaling, current tahimik lang" 😂
Star: VL↑ (×√3) | IL→ (same) | Neutral wire: PRESENT
△ "DIVA" — Delta Config Trick
Para sa Delta (Δ), tandaan ang "DIVA":
Delta = Icurrent nag-multiply (IL = √3 × Iph) — Diva yung current!
Voltage pareho (VL = Vph) — Same voltage!
Awalang neutral wire

Sa Delta: "Koryente ang nagmamagaling, voltage tahimik lang" 🦋
Delta: VL→ (same) | IL↑ (×√3) | Neutral wire: ABSENT
🔄 "TATLO" — Y-Δ Conversion
TATLO ang multiplier para sa conversion:

Y → Delta: Z_Δ = 3 × Z_Y  → "Tatlong beses mas malaki ang Delta"
Delta → Y: Z_Y = Z_Δ / 3  → "Hatiin sa tatlo para maging Star"

Memory hook: "Delta ang mahal, tatlong beses ng Star" 💰
ZΔ = 3·ZY   |   ZY = ZΔ/3   |   TATLO palagi!
📐 "LAG = Late = Inductive"
Lagging PF = Nag-late si current = Inductive load (motors, coils)
Leading PF = Naunahan si current = Capacitive load (capacitors)
Unity PF = Sabay sila! = Resistive load (heaters)

Memory: "ELI the ICE man"
Emf Leads In inductors (ELI) = Lagging current
Icurrent CEads in Capacitors (ICE) = Leading current
Lagging → θ positive | Leading → θ negative | Unity → θ = 0°

🎯 Golden Rule: Sa board exam, kapag nakita mong PF = 0.8, agad alam mo: sin(θ) = 0.6, kasi 3-4-5 triangle sila! P:Q:S = 4:3:5. Memorize mo ito para mabilis!
🔺 Power Triangle Shortcuts
Common PF = 0.8 → 3-4-5 triangle:
P = 4k W, Q = 3k VAR, S = 5k VA → PF = 4/5 = 0.80 ✅

Common PF = 0.6 → 3-4-5 flipped:
P = 3k W, Q = 4k VAR, S = 5k VA → PF = 3/5 = 0.60 ✅

PF = 0.866 (cos30°) → 1-√3-2 triangle:
P = √3k, Q = 1k, S = 2k → PF = √3/2 = 0.866 ✅

PF = 0.707 (cos45°) → Isosceles:
P = Q (equal!) → PF = 1/√2 = 0.707 ✅
Sa board exam: PF = 0.8 → agad isipin "3-4-5 triangle!" 🚀
📚 Real ECE Board Mnemonics
Maxwell Relations — "Good Physicists Have Studied Under Very Fine Teachers"

Gibbs · Pressure · Helmholtz · Strain · Utility · Volume · Free energy · Temperature
Tip: Para sa 3-phase exam, mas importante ang Power Triangle mnemonics — ito ay bonus lang!
Twinkle Twinkle — 3-Phase Power Formula Song
"Twinkle twinkle little star, P equals I-squared R!"
Para sa single-phase: P = I²R
Para sa 3-phase: P = √3 × V_L × I_L × PF o P = 3 × V_ph × I_ph × PF
P₃φ = √3 · V_L · I_L · cos θ = 3 · V_ph · I_ph · cos θ
Board trick: Kung di mo matandaan kung saan ang √3, isipin: Line quantities → may √3; Phase quantities → wala!
HEMEC — 3-Phase Motor Losses Mnemonic
H-E-M-E-C — ang limang pangunahing losses sa electric machines:
Hysteresis Loss (iron loss, dahil sa alternating magnetic field)
Eddy Current Loss (iron loss, induced currents sa core)
Mechanical Loss (friction at windage)
Electrical/Copper Loss (I²R sa windings)
Core/Stray Loss (iba pang miscellaneous losses)
Total Losses = P_hysteresis + P_eddy + P_mechanical + P_copper + P_stray
HEMEC sounds like "may sakit" — para di malimutan na may losses ang lahat ng machine!
Wye-Delta Phase Trick — "Wye Not Delta?"
Wye (Y): V_line = √3 × V_phase  |  I_line = I_phase
Delta (Δ): V_line = V_phase  |  I_line = √3 × I_phase
Wye: √3 multiplies VOLTAGE   Delta: √3 multiplies CURRENT
Mnemonic: "Wye-Y ako nagdadagdag ng √3 sa Voltage, Delta naman sa Current."
Pinaka-common na board trap! Palaging i-check kung Wye o Delta connection bago mag-solve.
