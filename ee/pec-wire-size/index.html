🎯 Board Exam Alert! Wire sizing ay laging lumalabas sa EE board exam (Power Systems) at ECE (EST). Alamin ang PEC rules: 125% para sa continuous loads, 3% max VD sa branch circuits.
🔑 PEC Key Rules — Must Memorize
📏 Rule 1 — Ampacity (PEC Art. 3.10)
Wire ampacity dapat ≥ load current. Para sa continuous loads (3+ hours), multiply current × 125% bago mag-size.
📉 Rule 2 — Voltage Drop (PEC Art. 2.30)
Max VD sa branch circuit: 3%
Max VD sa feeder: 2%
Max VD total (feeder + branch): 5%
🌡 Rule 3 — Temperature Correction
PH ambient = 30–40°C. THHN rated 90°C pero often derated to 75°C. Sa board exam, gamitin ang 60°C column unless specified.
🔢 Rule 4 — Conduit Fill Derating
4–6 conductors in conduit: derate to 80%
7–9 conductors: derate to 70%
10–20 conductors: derate to 50%
⚡ Rule 5 — Minimum Wire Size
Branch circuits: minimum 14 AWG (1.5mm²)
Never go below 14 AWG for any fixed wiring
Extension cords: 16 AWG minimum (portable only)
🧮 Voltage Drop Formula
VD (volts) = (2 × K × I × L) / CM
VD% = (VD / V_source) × 100
Where:
K = 12.9 (Copper) · 21.2 (Aluminum)
I = current in amperes
L = one-way length in feet (convert: 1m = 3.281ft)
CM = circular mils of wire

Trick: Para sa metric (meters), gamitin:
VD = (ρ × 2L × I) / A
ρ = 1.72×10⁻⁸ Ω·m (Cu) · A = area sa m²
📝 Sample Board Exam Problems
Problem 1: A 20A continuous load needs sizing. What is the minimum wire?
✓ 20A × 125% = 25A design current
✓ 12 AWG THHN ampacity = 20A ❌ (too small)
✓ 10 AWG THHN ampacity = 30A ✅
Answer: 10 AWG
Problem 2: 12 AWG Cu, 20A, 30m one-way, 230V single phase. Find VD%.
✓ 12 AWG CM = 6,530 CM
✓ L = 30m × 3.281 = 98.43 ft
✓ VD = (2 × 12.9 × 20 × 98.43) / 6530 = 7.77V
✓ VD% = 7.77 / 230 = 3.38% — exceeds 3% ❌
Upsize to 10 AWG (CM = 10,380)
Problem 3 (Bataan context): BEPZ factory, 3-phase 400V, 50A load, 50m feeder. Min wire size?
✓ 50A × 125% = 62.5A (continuous)
✓ Next size up from 60A = 6 AWG THHN (65A ampacity)
BEPZ = Bataan Economic Processing Zone — common sa local examples
