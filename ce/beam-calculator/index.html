CE Board Exam Tips — Structural Analysis
Sample Problem 1 — Simply Supported UDL
Problem: Ang simply supported beam ay may span na 8 m at may UDL na 15 kN/m. Hanapin ang maximum moment, maximum shear, at maximum deflection. Gamitin: E = 200 GPa, I = 300 × 10⁶ mm⁴.
Step 1: Max Moment → M = wL²/8 = 15(8²)/8 = 120 kN·m
Step 2: Max Shear → V = wL/2 = 15(8)/2 = 60 kN (sa supports)
Step 3: δ = 5wL⁴/384EI → i-convert: w=15 N/mm, L=8000 mm, E=200000 MPa, I=300×10⁶ mm⁴
Step 3 cont: δ = 5(15)(8000⁴)/(384 × 200000 × 300×10⁶) = 26.7 mm
Sagot: M = 120 kN·m, V = 60 kN, δ = 26.7 mm
Sample Problem 2 — Cantilever with Point Load
Problem: Isang cantilever beam, L = 4 m, P = 20 kN sa free end. Hanapin M, V, at δ. E = 25 GPa, I = 150 × 10⁶ mm⁴.
Step 1: Max Moment (sa fixed end) → M = PL = 20 × 4 = 80 kN·m
Step 2: Max Shear → V = P = 20 kN (constant throughout)
Step 3: δ = PL³/3EI = 20×10³×(4000)³/(3×25000×150×10⁶) = 56.9 mm
Sagot: M = 80 kN·m, V = 20 kN, δ = 56.9 mm
Sign Convention — Dapat Malaman!
+M
Sagging — beam baluktot pababa (concave up) = POSITIVE moment
−M
Hogging — beam baluktot pataas (concave down) = NEGATIVE moment
+V
Left side ng cut: upward force = POSITIVE shear
−V
Left side ng cut: downward force = NEGATIVE shear
Common Mistakes — Iwasang Gawin!
Unit Conversion Error
Malaking mistake ang pagsasama ng kN/m at N/mm nang walang conversion. Para sa deflection formula: i-convert lahat sa N at mm o kN at m — consistent ka lang. 1 kN/m = 1 N/mm
Paglimot ng EI sa Denominator
Ang deflection formula ay laging may EI sa denominator. Mas mataas ang EI, mas maliit ang deflection. "DISME" — Deflection Is Smaller with More E.
Fixed-Fixed — End Moments vs Midspan
Sa fixed-fixed beam, maximum moment ay sa mga fixed ends, hindi sa midspan. Midspan moment = wL²/24 (para sa UDL), ends = wL²/12.
Propped Cantilever — Statically Indeterminate
Hindi mo ma-solve ito gamit lang ang statics. Kailangan ng compatibility equation o three-moment theorem. Sa board exam, i-memorize ang reactions: Ra = 3wL/8 (simple end), Rb = 5wL/8 (fixed end).

Shear Diagram Rules
Concentrated load → vertical jump sa shear diagram
UDL → slanted/inclined line sa shear diagram
Walang load → horizontal line sa shear diagram
Zero shear → location ng maximum moment!
Ang integral ng shear diagram = moment diagram
Taglish Mnemonics — Matandaan Mo Na!
"Sa Simply Supported, WALONG Bahagi"
→ Simply Supported + UDL: M = wL²/8
"WALO" = 8 sa denominator. Tandaan: Walo para sa W-shaped (distributed) load sa SS beam.
"CANtilever = KALAHATI lang ng SS"
→ Cantilever UDL: M = wL²/2 — apat na beses na mas malaki kaysa SS!
Kaya mag-ingat — ang cantilever ay mas mapanganib pagdating sa moments. "Double the danger, half the constraint."
"DISME" — Deflection Is Smaller with More E
→ δ ∝ 1/EI — mas mataas ang EI, mas maliit ang deflection.
Gamitin ang matibay na materyales (steel > concrete > wood) para mabawasan ang deflection.
"5-P-8" para sa Simply Supported + 5wL⁴/384EI
→ Coefficient: 5, denominator: 384 EI
5÷384 ≈ 0.01302 — i-memorize ito para mabilis ang computation sa board.
"Fixed-Fixed = Dalawang beses na tigas"
→ Fixed-Fixed ay statically indeterminate sa degree 2.
Moments sa ends: wL²/12 (dalawang beses na mas maliit kaysa cantilever na wL²/6).
Deflection: wL⁴/384EI — same coefficient sa SS!
"SHEAR = REACTION minus LOAD"
Para makuha ang shear sa kahit saang punto:
V(x) = Ra − w·x (para sa UDL)
Ang zero shear ay nangyayari sa x = Ra/w — doon ang maximum moment!
"Distributed load = SLANTED shear"
Shear diagram rules mnemonic:
Distributed → Diagonal (slanted line)
Concentrated → Change (vertical jump)
Nothing → No change (horizontal)
Casio fx-991ES/EX PLUS Techniques
MODE 5-1: SOLVE Mode — Para sa Unknown Reactions
1 Mag-press ng [MODE][5][1] para sa Equation Solver. Para sa simply supported beam na may UDL: i-type ang equation Ra + Rb - w*L = 0
2 I-store ang known values: w → [ALPHA][A], L → [ALPHA][B]. Gamitin ang CALC para sa repeated evaluation.
3 Para sa moment equation: Ra*L - w*L*L/2 = 0, SOLVE para sa Ra. Automatic ang Rb = wL - Ra.
MODE 8: EQN Mode — Para sa Fixed-Fixed (Simultaneous Equations)
1 Mag-press ng [MODE][8][1] para sa 2 unknowns o [MODE][8][2] para sa 3 unknowns.
2 Para sa fixed-fixed beam: dalawang equilibrium equations → ang calculator ay mag-sosolve ng Ma at Mb (end moments) automatically.
3 I-enter ang coefficients ng bawat equation row by row. Press [=] pagkatapos ng bawat value. Lalabas ang solution agad.
Variable Memory A–F: I-store ang E at I
1 I-store ang E: Type 200000 (MPa) → press [SHIFT][RCL][A] (stores in A). Gamitin ang [ALPHA][A] sa equations.
2 I-store ang I: Type 300E6 → press [SHIFT][RCL][B]. Ngayon sa deflection formula: 5*w*L^4÷(384*A*B)
3 Tip: A=E, B=I, C=L, D=w, E=P, F=spare. I-memorize ang layout mo para hindi ka malito sa board exam.
CALC Mode: Para sa Repeated Formula Evaluation
1 I-type ang formula gamit ang variables, e.g. 5*D*C^4÷(384*A*B). Press [CALC].
2 Mag-prompt ang calculator para sa bawat variable. I-enter ang values, press [=] pagkatapos ng bawat isa.
3 Time-saver: Sa susunod na problem, press [CALC] ulit — lalabas ang dating values bilang default. I-overwrite lang ang nagbago.
Support Type Quick Reference
PIN (Hinged) Support → 2 reactions
Horizontal (H) + Vertical (V) reactions. Walang moment reaction. Pwedeng mag-rotate ang beam. → Symbol: triangle with pin sa vertex.
ROLLER Support → 1 reaction
Vertical reaction lang. Pwedeng mag-move horizontally at mag-rotate. → Ginagamit sa isang end ng simply supported beam para payagan ang thermal expansion.
FIXED Support → 3 reactions
Horizontal + Vertical + Moment reactions. Hindi pwedeng mag-move o mag-rotate. → Ito ang nagdudulot ng statically indeterminate condition sa fixed-fixed beams.
"PSR" — Pin, Simple, Roller (3 types na laging nasa board)
Pin = 2 rxn | Simple (roller) = 1 rxn | Fixed = 3 rxn
Degree of Indeterminacy = Total Reactions − 3 (for 2D structure)
GEAS & Real Board Mnemonics
Mohs Hardness Scale: "The Girls Can Flirt And Other Queer Things Can Do"
Talc (1) · Gypsum (2) · Calcite (3) · Fluorite (4) · Apatite (5) · Orthoclase (6) · Quartz (7) · Topaz (8) · Corundum (9) · Diamond (10)
Tandaan: 1 = pinaka-malambot, 10 = pinaka-matigas. Relevant sa materials selection at mineralogy problems.
Spring Shear Stress: "64 Prisoners niRape ng 3 nurse"
→ τ = 64PR / πd³ (spring shear stress formula)
Ang numerator ay 64PR; denominator ay πd³. Huwag ipagpalit ang d (wire diameter) at D (mean coil diameter).
Hollow Shaft Shear: "1sang 6sing Taga-Divisoria, PI naDapa 4 times dahil walang dalang d d"
→ τ = 16TD / π(D⁴ − d⁴) (max shear stress on hollow shaft / circular ring)
T = torque, D = outer diameter, d = inner diameter. Pag solid shaft: τ = 16T / πD³
Max Shear Stress (Solid Shaft): "16 Torque over Pi D cubed"
→ τ_max = 16T / πD³
"Sixteen Torque, Pi D-cube" — i-chant ito para matandaan. Board exam constant: pag solid circular shaft at torsion problem, ito ang formula.
