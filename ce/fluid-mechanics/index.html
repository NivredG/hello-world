Unit Traps — Pinakamalaking Pagkakamali sa Board

  • Diameter vs Radius: Manning's at Darcy-Weisbach uses hydraulic radius R = A/P, hindi ang diameter. Madalas maling gamitin ang D/2 as R directly sa rectangular channel!
  • Q: L/s vs m³/s: Divide by 1000. 50 L/s = 0.050 m³/s
  • Diameter: mm vs m: Darcy-Weisbach needs D in meters. 200 mm = 0.200 m
  • Slope S: Manning's needs dimensionless S (m/m). 1:1000 = 0.001
  • Pressure: kPa vs Pa: ρgh gives Pa. Divide by 1000 para sa kPa.
  • n exponent: Manning's has R^(2/3) at S^(1/2) — gamitin ang calculator nang tama!

Sample Problem — Manning's Open Channel

A trapezoidal canal has b=3m, y=1.5m, z=1.5, S=0.0008, n=0.025. Find Q.
Solution:
A = (b + zy)y = (3 + 1.5×1.5)(1.5) = (3+2.25)(1.5) = 7.875 m²
P = b + 2y√(1+z²) = 3 + 2(1.5)√(1+2.25) = 3 + 3√3.25 = 3 + 5.408 = 8.408 m
R = A/P = 7.875/8.408 = 0.9366 m
V = (1/0.025)(0.9366^0.667)(0.0008^0.5) = 40 × 0.9579 × 0.02828 = 1.082 m/s
Q = VA = 1.082 × 7.875 = 8.52 m³/s

Sample Problem — Darcy-Weisbach Pipe Flow

Steel pipe D=200mm, L=500m, Q=40 L/s, f=0.02, ν=1×10⁻⁶. Find hf at Re.
Solution:
A = π(0.2)²/4 = 0.03142 m²
V = Q/A = 0.040/0.03142 = 1.273 m/s
Re = VD/ν = 1.273×0.2/1×10⁻⁶ = 254,600 → Turbulent
hf = f(L/D)(V²/2g) = 0.02×(500/0.2)×(1.273²/19.62) = 0.02×2500×0.08258 = 4.13 m
ΔP = γ·hf = 9.81×4.13 = 40.5 kPa

⚠️

Most Common Board Exam Errors

  • Using R = D/2 (radius) instead of hydraulic radius R = A/P
  • Forgetting to convert Q from L/s to m³/s before computing V = Q/A
  • Using g = 10 m/s² when the problem gives exact values — use 9.81!
  • Manning's: wrong unit for S — dapat m/m (dimensionless), hindi percent
  • Bernoulli: forgetting head loss hf or pump head Hp in the energy equation
  • Hydrostatic force: using centroid depth as pressure, not as location of force application
  • Center of pressure palaging below ang centroid (for non-uniform pressure)

Optimal Hydraulic Sections

Para sa maximum discharge with minimum area (board exam favorites):
  • Rectangular: b = 2y (half-square), R = y/2
  • Trapezoidal: z = 1/√3 ≈ 0.577 (60° side slopes), R = y/2
  • Circular: full flow (y=D) gives max velocity; y≈0.938D gives max Q
  • Rule: Palaging R = y/2 ang optimal trapezoid at rectangle!
Best hydraulic trapezoidal section for Q=5m³/s, n=0.013, S=0.001?
Use z=1/√3, set b=2y/√3, solve Manning's for y iteratively or via CalTech SOLVE.
Mnemonic — Manning's n Factors
"MANNINGS"
Material (pipe/channel material) · Age & condition · Non-uniformity of section · Natural obstructions · Irregularity of alignment · Near banks vegetation · Grade (slope change) · Sediment & siltation — Lahat ito nakakaapekto sa n!
Mnemonic — Flow Rate Formula
"QVA — Kumuha ng Velocity, Multiply sa Area"
Q = V × A
Q = discharge (m³/s) · V = velocity (m/s) · A = cross-sectional area (m²)
Tagalog: "Ang Q ay katulad ng isang relo — lahat ay dumadaan sa iisang oras at lugar."
Mnemonic — Hydrostatic Pressure
"Pagala-gala sa Gripo, Hindi libre"
P = ρgh = pressure · density · gravity · depth
Interpretation: Mas malalim ang tubig, mas mataas ang presyon. "Hindi libre" = may bayad ang pressure sa mas malalim! Ganyan din sa MWSS billing — mas mataas ang floor, mas mababang pressure.
Mnemonic — Bernoulli's Principle
"Bernoulli's Batas: Ang Energy ay Nananatili"
P/γ + V²/2g + z = constant
Tatlong Components:
· Pressure head P/γ — "ang lakas ng tubig"
· Velocity head V²/2g — "ang bilis ng tubig"
· Elevation head z — "ang taas ng tubig"
Kapag bumilis ang tubig → bumaba ang pressure (Bernoulli!)
Mnemonic — Reynolds Number
"Laminar = Maayos, Turbulent = Gulo"
Re = VD/ν
Re < 2000: Laminar — "parang linya ng mga estudyante sa pagkain, maayos!"
Re 2000–4000: Transition — "break time, magulo-gulo na"
Re > 4000: Turbulent — "dismissal time sa Bataan, lahat gulo!"
Laminar: f = 64/Re (exact formula!)
Mnemonic — EGL vs HGL
"HGL laging nasa Below ng EGL"
Energy Grade Line (EGL) = total head = P/γ + V²/2g + z
Hydraulic Grade Line (HGL) = piezometric head = P/γ + z
Difference: Velocity head V²/2g — kaya palaging mataas ang EGL kaysa HGL.
Kapag stationary ang fluido (V=0) → EGL = HGL
Mnemonic — Darcy-Weisbach
"FLV² — Friction Loss Versus 2g Diameter"
hf = f × (L/D) × (V²/2g)
F = friction factor · L/D = pipe length ratio · V²/2g = velocity head
"Mas mahaba at mas maliit ang pipe, mas mataas ang head loss. Katulad ng trapik sa EDSA — mas mahabang daan, mas maraming friction!"

Casio fx-991ES/EX PLUS — CalTech Techniques

  • Manning's Q: Store n→A, S→B, b→C, y→D. Then compute: (1÷A)×((C+D)×D÷(C+2D))^(2÷3)×B^(1÷2)×((C+D)×D) — pindutin CALC para magpalit ng values
  • R^(2/3) shortcut: Type R, then press x^y, type 2÷3. Gamit ang parenthesis para sa fractions!
  • SOLVE mode para sa unknown D: Pumunta sa MODE 5 (EQN) o gamitin ang SHIFT+CALC (SOLVE). I-set ang equation, lagyan ng X ang unknown, pindutin SOLVE.
  • TABLE mode para sa Q vs D curve: MODE 7, i-type ang Manning's formula sa terms ng X (depth y). I-set ang start/end/step values. Makikita ang tabulated results!
  • Store values: Type value, press SHIFT+RCL+letter (A-F). Mag-imbak ng n, S, b para mabilis ang kalkulasyon.
  • Darcy-Weisbach SOLVE: Store f,L,D values. For unknown Q: express V=Q/(πD²/4), substitute sa hf formula. Gamit SOLVE para sa Q.
  • Re verification: After computing V, multiply by D (in m), divide by ν (1.004e-6). Press EXP para sa scientific notation sa display.

Board Exam Time Management Tips

  • Una, identify ang given at ang unknown. I-draw ang diagram agad (free body, control volume, channel cross-section).
  • Check ang units bago kalkulahin — unit conversion muna sa scratch paper.
  • Sa multiple choice: mag-eliminate ng obviously wrong answers (negative flow, etc.). Pag nalito, gawin ang simplest case (rectangular, laminar).
  • Manning's problems: compute A at P muna bago R. Huwag laktawan ang R = A/P.
  • Bernoulli: i-draw ang control volume at label ang dalawang points. Laging isulat ang buong equation bago mag-substitute.
  • Check ang answer: makatuwiran ba? (Velocity <10 m/s para sa normal pipes, Q positive, P positive.)
GEAS Mnemonics — Physics & Thermodynamics
"Pure Virgins have Nice Round Tits"
→ PV = nRT — Ideal Gas Law
P = pressure · V = volume · n = moles · R = gas constant (8.314 J/mol·K) · T = temperature (Kelvin)
Board Tip: I-convert lagi ang temperature sa Kelvin: T(K) = T(°C) + 273.15. Gamitin R = 0.08206 L·atm/mol·K para sa atmospheric pressure problems.
GEAS Mnemonics — Fluid Properties
"What's new (nu)? mu over rho"
→ ν = μ/ρ — Kinematic viscosity = Dynamic viscosity ÷ Density
ν (nu) = kinematic viscosity (m²/s) · μ (mu) = dynamic viscosity (Pa·s) · ρ = density (kg/m³)
Standard water @ 20°C: μ = 1.002×10⁻³ Pa·s · ν = 1.004×10⁻⁶ m²/s
GEAS Mnemonics — Gas Laws
"Pag may CaViT GiPiT"
→ Charles' Law: V ∝ T (constant pressure) — Volume at Temperature
→ Gay-Lussac's Law: P ∝ T (constant volume) — Pressure at Temperature
Combined: Boyle's (P∝1/V) + Charles' + Gay-Lussac's = Ideal Gas Law. "CaViT" = Charles Volume-Temperature. "GiPiT" = Gay-Lussac Pressure-Temperature.
GEAS Mnemonics — Thermodynamics
"q = CATMan" — Specific Heat Formula
→ q = Cv × ΔT × m
q = heat energy · Cv = specific heat · ΔT = temp change · m = mass
Para sa tubig: Cv = 4.186 kJ/kg·°C. Gamitin sa calorimetry problems at heat exchanger calculations sa board exam.
GEAS Mnemonics — Wave Physics
"Q: What's new (nu)? A: c/lambda"
→ ν = c/λ — Wave frequency = speed of light ÷ wavelength
ν (nu) = frequency (Hz) · c = speed of light (3×10⁸ m/s) · λ (lambda) = wavelength (m)
Note: Same "nu" symbol bilang kinematic viscosity — huwag malito! Sa wave problems, ν = frequency. Sa fluid problems, ν = kinematic viscosity.
