DPWH vs ACI — Key Differences
DPWH Volume Method — Simplified, field-use. Uses fixed proportions per class. Hindi na kailangan ng trial mix kung class-based ang project.

ACI 211 — Engineered approach. Considers slump, aggregate size, w/c ratio. Kailangan ng laboratory trial mix para sa final proportions.

DPWH is used for government road/bridge/building projects. ACI 211 is used for structural design and board exams.
!
Common Mistakes — Huwag Kamutin!
1. Forgetting sand bulking — Basang buhangin ay lumalaki ng 20–30%. Palaging gamitin ang dry loose volume sa calculations.

2. Wrong unit weight — Plain concrete = 24 kN/m³, Reinforced = 23.5 kN/m³. Huwag ipagpalit!

3. Confusing f'c and fy — f'c = compressive strength ng concrete (MPa). fy = yield strength ng rebar (MPa). Different materials!

4. Volume vs. weight mix — Class A "1:2:4" ay volume ratio, hindi weight ratio. Iba ang density ng bawat component.

5. Forgetting standard deviation — Required f'cr = f'c + 1.34s (ACI 318). Add margin for quality control.
✓
Quality Control Tests
Slump Test (ASTM C143) — Field test para sa workability. Fill cone in 3 layers, tamp 25x each. Lift cone, measure settlement (slump).

Cylinder Test (ASTM C39) — 150mm dia × 300mm height. Cure 28 days. Test compressive strength. Minimum 2 cylinders per 50 m³ pour.

Schmidt Hammer (Rebound) — Non-destructive test. Estimate strength ng existing concrete. Hindi kasingsaktong cylinder test pero mabilis.

DPWH QC Requirement — At least 1 slump test at 1 set of cylinders per 50 m³ or per day of pour.

Key Formulas para sa Board Exam
Required average strength:
f'cr = f'c + 1.34s (known variability)
f'cr = f'c + 2.33s − 3.5 (alternative)

Water-cement ratio (Abrams Law):
f'c = A / B^(w/c) where A≈96.5, B≈8.2 (ksi units)

Absolute volume method:
Vol = Weight / (SG × 1000)

Remember: Sum of absolute volumes = 1 m³
Taglish Mnemonics — Madaling Matandaan
"CWSG" — Mix Order
Cement, Water, Sand, Gravel — ang tamang pagkakasunod-sunod ng pag-iisip sa mix design. "Cement Water Sinabugan ng Gravel."
"Mas mababa ang w/c, mas malakas ang concrete"
Lower water-cement ratio = stronger concrete. W/c 0.40 > 0.55 in terms of strength. "Tubig = kahinaan ng concrete."
"1 bag = 40 kilo = 28.3 litro"
1 bag ng semento = 40 kg weight = 28.3 liters volume (SG=3.15). I-memorize: 40 at 28.3.
"Class AA = Awesome Arko"
DPWH Class AA gamit sa Columns (Arco/Arc) at Arches. f'c = 27.6 MPa. "Pinakamalakas sa lahat ng class."
"Slump = Tamad na concrete"
Mataas na slump = maraming tubig = madaling mag-pour PERO mas mahina. "High slump, weak dump." Limit slump per element type.
"f'c = Field concrete | fy = Field rebar Yield"
Huwag ipagpalit: f'c = compressive strength ng concrete (MPa). fy = yield strength ng reinforcing steel (MPa). Iba ang material!
"23.5 at 24" — Unit Weights
23.5 kN/m³ = reinforced concrete. 24 kN/m³ = plain/mass concrete. "Ang reinforcement ay nagpapagaan (−0.5)." Hindi totoo pero makatutulong sa mnemonic.
"3-2-1 ang DPWH Class A"
Class A = 1 cement : 2 sand : 4 gravel. "Hindi 3-2-1, kundi 1-2-4 — reverse mo!" Trick para matandaan ang sequence.

GEAS & Real Board Mnemonics — Chemistry
"Do what you Oughter, add acid to water!"
NEVER add water to acid — ang acid ang dapat ibuhos sa tubig, hindi ang kabaligtaran. Exothermic reaction: pag naibuhos ang tubig sa acid, maaring mag-splatter at mapaso ka. Acid to water = safe. Water to acid = accident!
"Acid to Water: All is Well. Water to Acid: What an Accident!"
Dalawang bersyon ng parehong panuntunan. Sa concrete mix context: kapag gumagamit ng chemical admixtures o cleaning agents, laging dilute sa tubig bago gamitin. Relevant din sa curing compound application.
Periodic Table Group 1: "HaLiNa Kayo Rubi at Cesar sa France"
Hydrogen · Lithium · Natrium (Sodium) · Kalium (Potassium) · Rbidium · Csium · Francium
Alkali metals — Group 1. Highly reactive, pwedeng mag-react sa tubig. Relevant sa concrete chemistry at soil corrosion problems.
Group 2 Alkaline Earth: "Be, MgCano kay Sr. BaRat?"
Beryllium · Mgnesium · Calcium · Srontium · Barium · Radium
Calcium at Magnesium ang pinakacommon sa concrete chemistry (cement hydration, aggregate reaction). Ca(OH)₂ = portlandite ang byproduct ng cement hydration.
"OIL RIG" — Redox Reactions
Oxidation Is Loss · Reduction Is Gain
Importanteng concept sa corrosion ng reinforcing steel. Kapag nag-corrode ang rebar: Fe → Fe²⁺ (oxidation = nawawalan ng electrons). Ang chloride attack sa concrete ay nagdudulot ng corrosion ng rebar.
"RED CAT, AN OX" — Electrochemistry
Reduction at Cathode · Anode = Oxidation
Para sa rebar corrosion: ang cathode ay ang lugar kung saan nag-rereduce ang oxygen (O₂ + 2H₂O + 4e⁻ → 4OH⁻). Ang anode ay kung saan nag-corrode ang bakal. Cathodic protection ay kontrahin ito gamit ang impressed current.
