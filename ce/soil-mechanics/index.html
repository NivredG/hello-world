CE Board Exam — Geotechnical Tips

Para sa CE board exam sa Pilipinas — Taglish tips mula sa experience ng mga PRC passer

Tip 1 — Alam mo ba ang tamang sequence?

  • Basahin muna lahat ng given: unit system, may GWT ba?
  • I-check: submerged ba ang soil? Gamitin γ' = γsat − 9.81
  • Ilagay sa formula: FS laging huling i-apply sa qu
  • Mag-round ng 2 decimal places para sa final answer

Tip 2 — Common na Mali sa Board

  • Hindi ginagamit ang γ' para sa submerged bearing capacity
  • Nakalimot na i-subtract ang container mass sa water content
  • Mali ang log base: dapat log10, hindi ln para sa Sc
  • Nakakalito ang LL at PL sa LI formula: LI = (w − PL) / PI
  • Square at circular footing — may shape factor na different sa strip

Sample Problem 1 — Terzaghi Bearing Capacity (Square Footing)

Given: B = 1.5m, Df = 1.2m, c = 0, φ = 30°, γ = 18 kN/m³, FS = 3

Nc = 37.16, Nq = 22.46, Nγ = 19.73 (φ=30°)
Square: qu = 1.3cNc + γDfNq + 0.4γBNγ
qu = 0 + (18)(1.2)(22.46) + 0.4(18)(1.5)(19.73)
qu = 484.7 + 213.1 = 697.8 kPa
qs = qu / FS = 697.8 / 3 = 232.6 kPa

Sample Problem 2 — Consolidation Settlement (Manila Bay clay)

Given: Cc = 0.42, eo = 1.15, H = 4m, σ'o = 60 kPa, Δσ = 40 kPa

Sc = [Cc/(1+eo)] × H × log₁₀[(σ'o+Δσ)/σ'o]
= [0.42/2.15] × 4 × log₁₀(100/60)
= 0.1953 × 4 × 0.2218
Sc = 0.173 m = 173 mm

Note: Manila Bay soft clay — mataas ang settlement dahil mataas ang eo at Cc

Tip 3 — PHIVOLCS at DPWH Context

  • DPWH: Minimum FS = 3 para sa bearing capacity ng public structures
  • PHIVOLCS fault zone: mag-ingat sa seismic-induced settlement
  • Manila Bay area: expect soft clay, mataas na settlement, shallow water table
  • Bataan peninsula: volcanic ash deposits, variable φ at c
  • NSP (National Structural Code): punching shear check for isolated footings

Tip 4 — Water Content Formula Check

  • w = (Mw/Md) × 100% — mass ng tubig sa mass ng dry soil
  • Mw = Mwet − Md (huwag kalimutan i-minus ang container!)
  • Mdry = Md − Mc (net dry mass)
  • Pag may container: Mw = Mwet − Md (container cancels out)

Mnemonics & CalTech Tips

Casio fx-991ES/EX PLUS techniques + Taglish mnemonics para sa board exam

MNEMONIC

"LL − PL = PI" → Leche minus Patis = Pulutan

Liquid Limit minus Plastic Limit equals Plasticity Index. Tandaan: LL > PL palagi, kaya positive ang PI. Pag negative ang PI mo — may mali sa computation!

PI = LL − PL  |  LI = (wn − PL) / PI
MNEMONIC

"Terzaghi's Tatlong Termino"

Tatlong bahagi ng bearing capacity formula — huwag kalimutan kahit isa!

qu = c·Nc + γDf·Nq + 0.5γB·Nγ

Term 1 = Cohesion | Term 2 = Depth | Term 3 = Width

MNEMONIC

"CH = Crying High" — CL = Crying Low

CH = Clay, High plasticity (LL ≥ 50, above A-line) — tulad ng Manila Bay clay, mataas ang settlement, nakakaiyak!
CL = Clay, Low plasticity (LL < 50, above A-line) — mas maayos, mas mura ang treatment.

MNEMONIC

"Submerged? Gamma Prime = γsat − γw"

Pag nasa tubig ang soil (below GWT), gamitin ang effective unit weight. Huwag gamitin ang γ sa bearing capacity calculation pag submerged ang base ng footing!

γ' = γsat − 9.81 kN/m³
MNEMONIC

"SAME fs=3 palagi" — Board Exam Default

Pag walang ibinigay na FS sa problem, gamitin ang FS = 3 para sa bearing capacity. Safe at DPWH-compliant.

qs = qu / FS
MNEMONIC

"Soft Clay = Malaking Settlement"

Mataas ang Cc at eo ng soft clay (Manila Bay: Cc≈0.4, eo≈1.2) → malaking Sc. Pag mataas ang Sc, mag-isip ng preloading o pile foundation.

Sc = [Cc/(1+eo)] × H × log10[(σ'o+Δσ)/σ'o]
MNEMONIC

"e = Void ratio, n = Porosity — magkaibang formula!"

e = Vv/Vs  |  n = Vv/V = e/(1+e)

Hindi interchangeable! n laging mas maliit sa e. n palaging < 1 (fraction), e puwedeng >1 para sa malambot na clay.

GEAS MNEMONICS — Chemistry / Physics

Reactivity Series: "Pop Stars Can Make All Zombies In Town Lose His Cool"

K · Na · Ca · Mg · Al · Zn · Fe · Sn · Pb · H · Cu
(Potassium, Sodium, Calcium, Magnesium, Aluminum, Zinc, Iron, Tin, Lead, Hydrogen, Copper)
Relevant sa soil contamination problems at corrosion ng buried steel pipes. Metals sa kaliwa ng H ay nag-rereact sa tubig; metals sa kanan ng H ay hindi nag-rereact sa dilute acids.

Reactivity: K > Na > Ca > Mg > Al > Zn > Fe > Sn > Pb > H > Cu
GEAS MNEMONICS — Electrochemistry

"OIL RIG" — Oxidation Is Loss, Reduction Is Gain

Ang pinaka-importanteng mnemonic sa redox reactions. Para sa soil chemistry at corrosion ng buried steel:
Ang iron (Fe) ay nag-o-oxidize → Fe²⁺ + 2e⁻ (rumal/rust). Mas acidic o chloride-rich ang soil, mas mabilis ang corrosion.
Cathodic protection ng buried pipelines ay gumagamit ng sacrificial anode (zinc o magnesium).

OIL RIG: Oxidation = Loss of electrons | Reduction = Gain of electrons
GEAS MNEMONICS — Electrochemistry

"RED CAT" — Reduction at Cathode

Sa electrochemical soil testing at corrosion protection:
RED CAT = Reduction at Cathode (nag-aakkumula ng electrons, nabibigyan ng proteksyon)
Ang anode ay kung saan nag-corrode ang metal (sacrifice anode method). Board exam application: cathodic protection ng buried pipelines at rebars.

Cathode: reduction (protected) | Anode: oxidation (corrodes)
GEAS MNEMONICS — Thermodynamics

"Pure Virgins have Nice Round Tits" — PV = nRT

Ideal Gas Law — applicable sa soil gas problems, vapor pressure sa pore water, at landfill gas calculations.
P = absolute pressure (Pa) · V = volume (m³) · n = moles · R = 8.314 J/mol·K · T = temperature in Kelvin
I-convert lagi: T(K) = T(°C) + 273.15. Para sa vapor pressure ng pore water sa unsaturated soils.

PV = nRT  |  Para sa landfill gas (CH₄, CO₂) at soil vapor problems
GEAS MNEMONICS — Engineering Economy

"Thirty days hath September, April, June and November"

Para sa time-value of money calculations sa geotechnical projects (construction schedules, loan amortization):
September, April, June, November = 30 days. Lahat ng iba = 31 days (maliban February = 28 o 29).
Board exam: kapag may interest calculations sa project duration, i-count nang tama ang bilang ng araw sa bawat buwan.

Sept · Apr · Jun · Nov = 30 days  |  Feb = 28/29  |  Lahat ng iba = 31
