Mga Formula na Kailangan sa Board
4-Band Formula
R = (10×D₁ + D₂) × Multiplier
Tol = Gold(±5%) o Silver(±10%)
Halimbawa: Brown–Black–Red–Gold
R = (10×1 + 0) × 100 = 1,000 Ω = 1 kΩ ±5%
5-Band Formula
R = (100×D₁ + 10×D₂ + D₃) × Multiplier
Halimbawa: Brown–Black–Black–Red–Brown
R = (100×1 + 0 + 0) × 100 = 10,000 Ω = 10 kΩ ±1%
Resistors sa Series
R_total = R₁ + R₂ + R₃ + ···
Resistors sa Parallel
Para sa 2: R = (R₁ × R₂) ÷ (R₁ + R₂)
Para sa ≥3: 1/R = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂ + 1/R₃ + ···
Ohm's Law at Power
V = IR I = V/R R = V/I
P = IV = I²R = V²/R
Power ratings: 1/8W · 1/4W · 1/2W · 1W · 2W · 5W · 10W
Karaniwang Board Exam Questions
Q: Gold at Silver bilang Multiplier (hindi Tolerance)?
Gold bilang multiplier = ×0.1 (para sa values < 10 Ω)
Silver bilang multiplier = ×0.01 (para sa values < 1 Ω)
Halimbawa: Red–Gold = 2 × 0.1 = 0.2 Ω
Q: Ano ang sinasabi ng 6th band?
Band 6 = Temperature Coefficient (TC) sa ppm/°C
Brown = 100 ppm/°C · Red = 50 · Orange = 15 · Yellow = 25
Ibig sabihin: gaano nagbabago ang resistance per degree Celsius
Q: Ano ang E-series at bakit mahalaga?
E6 = 6 values/decade — ±20% tolerance
E12 = 12 values/decade — ±10% tolerance
E24 = 24 values/decade — ±5% tolerance ← pinakakaraniwan
E96 = 96 values/decade — ±1% tolerance ← precision
E192 = 192 values/decade — ±0.5% tolerance
Q: Paano decode ang SMD resistor code?
3-digit XYZ: R = XY × 10Z
4-digit XYZW: R = XYZ × 10W
R-notation: R ay decimal point (4R7 = 4.7 Ω)
EIA-96: 2-digit index + letter multiplier (01A = 100 Ω)
💡 Paalala: Sa PRC ECE Board, kadalasang tinatanong ang 4-band decode, parallel resistance, at power dissipation. Araling mabuti ang mga formula sa itaas!
