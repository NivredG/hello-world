Mga Formula
4-Band Resistor
R = (10×D1 + D2) × Multiplier
Tolerance = ±Gold(5%) or Silver(10%)
Halimbawa: Brown-Black-Red-Gold
R = (10×1 + 0) × 100 = 1,000 Ω = 1 kΩ ±5%
5-Band Resistor
R = (100×D1 + 10×D2 + D3) × Multiplier
Tolerance = band 5
Halimbawa: Brown-Black-Black-Red-Brown
R = (100×1 + 0 + 0) × 100 = 10,000 Ω = 10 kΩ ±1%
Resistors sa Series
R_total = R1 + R2 + R3 + ...
Resistors sa Parallel
Para sa 2 resistors:
R_total = (R1 × R2) / (R1 + R2)

Para sa marami:
1/R_total = 1/R1 + 1/R2 + 1/R3 + ...
Power Rating — Ohm's Law
V = IR    I = V/R    R = V/I

P = IV = I²R = V²/R
Tandaan: Karaniwan na power rating ng resistors — 1/8W, 1/4W, 1/2W, 1W, 2W, 5W, 10W
Mga Karaniwang Board Exam Questions
Q: Paano mabasa ang Gold at Silver bilang multiplier?
Gold = ×0.1 (para sa values na mas mababa sa 10Ω)
Silver = ×0.01 (para sa values na mas mababa sa 1Ω)
Halimbawa: Red-Gold = 2 × 0.1 = 0.2 Ω
Q: Paano mabasa ang 6-band resistor?
Band 1,2,3 = Digits · Band 4 = Multiplier
Band 5 = Tolerance · Band 6 = Temperature Coefficient
(TC unit: ppm/°C — ilang ohm ang nagbabago per degree Celsius)
Q: Ano ang preferred values (E-series)?
E6 = 6 values/decade (±20% tol)
E12 = 12 values/decade (±10% tol)
E24 = 24 values/decade (±5% tol) ← pinakakaraniwan
E96 = 96 values/decade (±1% tol) ← precision resistors
